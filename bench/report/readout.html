Speech-to-text on security jargon: readout

Jargon Bench · runs on 2026-10-04 (UTC) · prepared by Ky Gray

1. What we tested and why

Field techs and customers use brand and part names (Verkada, PoE, Wiegand, NVR) that general speech-to-text often mishears, and a misheard word becomes a wrong ticket. We measured how often Deepgram and ElevenLabs get those terms right on 44 recorded field-style sentences, with and without each provider's keyterm boosting.

4. Latency and cost

Deepgram: median 119 ms default, 128 ms boosted; $0.26 → $0.34 per audio hour at list price. ElevenLabs: median 451 ms default, 472 ms boosted; $0.22 → $0.27 per audio hour at list price. Latency was measured from a home connection and includes upload.

5. Recommendation and caveats

  • Turn on keyterm boosting for field audio. On recorded speech it raised jargon accuracy from 62.1% to 89.7% with Deepgram and from 79.3% to 95.4% with ElevenLabs.
  • The cost was small: median latency changed by 9 ms and 21 ms, and list price rose from $0.26 to $0.34 and from $0.22 to $0.27 per audio hour.
  • On this dataset ElevenLabs was more accurate (95.4% vs 89.7% boosted) and Deepgram answered faster (median 128 ms vs 472 ms).
  • Review the boost list for terms that sound like everyday words: boosting wrote "Axis" and "supervisory" where it wasn't said (5 clip-level cases across all audio).
